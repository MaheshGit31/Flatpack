    4 Why it matters

    Works without reading

    A picture of a hand pressing a button needs no vocabulary. Nobody has to decode a paragraph to take their medicine.

    Works in any language

    The same guide is right in every country. Hand it over, no translation, no app, no account.

    Works when you're overwhelmed

    One action per panel. Short, calm, in order. Exactly what a stressed brain can follow.

    Made for kids, people with low literacy, people new to a country, and anyone stressed at a form, a medicine, or a box of parts.

    5 How it's built

    1. Readvision model reads the photo
    3. Plan4 to 8 steps, one action each
    5. DrawNano Banana, in parallel
    7. Checka second pass inspects every panel

    The critic loop: each panel is checked for the step it claims, stray text, and a drifting character. Failures are redrawn once. Tap "this isn't what I see" and only the affected steps are redrawn.
